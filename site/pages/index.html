Questions

Is it really free?
The software is MIT licensed and designed to fit the Cloudflare Workers Free plan at hobby scale: a few sites, thousands of hits a day. Cloudflare bills you directly if you outgrow the free tier; 404less has no fees of its own.
What does the snippet collect?
Only on a page it detects as a 404: the URL, the referrer, and the user agent. It sets no cookies and does nothing on pages that aren’t a 404. Hits are rate-limited per site and pruned after 90 days by default.
Will it slow my site down?
The script is about 14 KB, loads deferred, and only acts on 404 pages. The hit is sent with sendBeacon, so it never blocks navigation.
How is this different from Ahrefs or Oh Dear?
Those are broad suites that include a crawl. 404less does one job end to end: it sees the 404s visitors actually hit, finds the rest with a crawler, suggests the fix, hands you the redirect rule, and keeps the list clean with resolve/ignore and automatic reopening. And you host it yourself.
Can I monitor several sites?
Yes. Sites belong to a team; each has its own key, filters, crawl schedule and alert settings. The default cap is five sites per team to protect free-tier limits, and it’s a one-line setting to raise.
