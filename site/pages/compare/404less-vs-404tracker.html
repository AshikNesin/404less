404less vs 404tracker.

Both will tell a coding agent about broken links. 404tracker crawls a site you do not host the checker for. 404less records the 404s your visitors hit, on a Worker in your Cloudflare account.

Their pricing page also lists a Custom tier (“let’s talk”, hello@404tracker.com) and extra credits: $2 per 1,000 pages, $5 per 10,000 API calls. CSV and PDF export are on every column, including Free. The homepage’s free scan line is “Up to 50 pages · No credit card needed”, which matches the Free column.

The homepage also says “Free forever with 10 scans/month. Pro from $5/mo. Business from $19/mo.” That matches the table.

404less does not have a price. The software is MIT. You deploy it to your Cloudflare account. The Workers Free plan limits (requests, D1 writes, cron triggers) are Cloudflare’s, described on /self-host. The app default MAX_SITES_PER_TEAM is 5. Crawl pages per run default to 100, with a per-site cap of 500. Those are variables, not a bill.

What each one is for

Where it runs Their service. “No signup needed for your first check.” Your Cloudflare account. One-click deploy or pnpm deploy:worker.

Signup-free scan Yes, capped at 50 pages. No public scanner in this repo. You run the instance.

Their homepage says broken links make “SEO rankings drop” and that Google “penalizes sites with broken links” and “lowers your domain authority.” This page does not adopt that claim. Google’s status-code document says a URL that returns 404 is dropped from the index. That is about the URL, and it is a different statement from a sitewide penalty.

Both have an MCP server. They answer different questions.

404tracker’s homepage card says “MCP Server — Scan sites from Cursor or Claude Desktop — free, no signup.” The pricing table, on the same day, marks MCP unavailable on Free and included from Pro ($5/month) up. Those two sentences disagree. Use the pricing table if you are about to connect a client, and treat the homepage card as something to re-check.

404less MCP is four read-only tools against your data: list_sites, list_broken_links, get_suggestions, get_link_details. Setup is the Claude Code and Cursor guide. The server does not scan an arbitrary URL you paste. It reads the sites on the team that owns the API key.

A practical split

Use 404tracker’s free scan when you want a 50-page look at a site today and you do not want an account. Use their paid plans when you want them to crawl on a schedule and email you, and you are fine with the page caps in the table.

Use 404less when the site is yours to deploy onto, you want the 404s real visitors hit (including URLs nobody links anymore), and you want a redirect rule in the format of the host. You take on Cloudflare’s quotas and the upgrade path in the repo.

The two can sit next to each other. A one-off scan does not replace a 404 template, and a self-hosted log does not give you a no-signup scan of someone else’s domain.
